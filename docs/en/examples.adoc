== Examples of sign configurations

This appendix describes examples of variable message signs (VMS) in the static publication. Each example
has an image marking the described parts and their indexes, a description of the basic concept and a
sample. The corresponding dynamic publications (samples `dynamic-…`) are described in the documentation of
the `cz-ndic_d2-vms-status` format.

The samples are part of the specification package `{schema-uri}.zip` (folder `samples`).

=== Information cantilever or gantry with ZPI

A real controller from the NDIC table (30 Sep 2026): a TSK cantilever in Wilsonova street (Prague) with a
traffic information device – a PDZ and a PIT with three lines.

image::ex-02.png[Cantilever with ZPI,width=600]

* VMS controller (`vms:VmsController`): 1×, table `CzechVmsTable-fixedVms`
* VMS (`vms:Vms`; `roadsideCantileverMounted`, `vmsType` = `simpleMatrixSign`, `vmsTypeCode` =
  `variableInformationPanel`): 1× dynamic sign on the cantilever for the whole carriageway [vms:1]
* Display areas (`vms:DisplayArea`): [vms:1] 1× pictogram (with an implicit supplementary panel) and 1× text with 3 lines
* Location: coordinates, chainage of a local road (`roadName`) and a Global Network section

=== Lane control prism signs

A real controller from the NDIC table (30 Sep 2026): a pair of prism variable signs on both sides of the
D3 motorway in the direction of České Budějovice. A prism sign displays one of three fixed symbols by
rotating its prisms, e.g. a maximum speed of 150{nbsp}km/h (`B20a_150`) or the end of the restriction
(`B20b_150`).

image::ex-10.png[Pair of variable signs on both sides of the road (illustrative),width=600]

* VMS controller (`vms:VmsController`): 1×, table `CzechVmsTable-fixedVms`
* VMS (`vms:Vms`; `roadsideMounted`, `vmsType` = `rotatingPrismSign`, `vmsTypeCode` = `variableMessageSign`,
  `dedicatedUsage` = `laneControlSystem`): 2× sign along the road – on the left [vms:1] and on the right [vms:2]
  (`positionOnCarriageway`)
* Display areas (`vms:DisplayArea`): [vms:1,2] 1× pictogram

=== Other controller configurations (without sample)

The following examples show how other typical controller configurations are described. There is no
sample for them.

==== Information gantry with ZPI and lane control

image::ex-11.png[Information gantry with ZPI and lane control,width=600]

* VMS controller (`vms:VmsController`): 1×
* VMS (`vms:Vms`; `gantryMounted`):
** 2× VMS on the left and right post including flashers for the whole carriageway [vms:1,5]
** 1× ZPI on the gantry for the whole carriageway [vms:2]
** 2× variable signal for lane control (`laneNumber`) [vms:3,4]
* Display areas (`vms:DisplayArea`):
** [vms:1,3,4,5] 1× pictogram
** [vms:2] 1× pictogram (with an implicit supplementary panel) and 1× text

==== Lane control gantry with static signs

image::ex-12.png[Lane control gantry with static signs,width=600]

* VMS controller (`vms:VmsController`): 1×
* VMS (`vms:Vms`; `gantryMounted`):
** 2× non-variable ("metal") information sign on the left and right along the road [vms:1,7]
** 3× PDZ above the lane centre lines (`laneNumber`) [vms:2,4,6]
** 2× warning or prohibitory PDZ for the whole carriageway [vms:3,5]
* Display areas (`vms:DisplayArea`):
** [vms:1,3,5,7] 1× pictogram
** [vms:2,4,6] 2× pictogram (main sign and secondary signal)
* Non-variable signs are described in the same way as variable ones; in the dynamic publication their
  content is always the same.

==== Gantry with fully configurable signs

image::ex-13.png[Gantry with fully configurable signs,width=600]

* VMS controller (`vms:VmsController`): 1×
* VMS (`vms:Vms`; `gantryMounted`): 2× VMS on the gantry for the whole carriageway [vms:1,2]
* Display areas (`vms:DisplayArea`): [vms:1,2] 1× pictogram; the content of the fully configurable area is
  described by the dynamic publication with an image (`pictogramDisplayUrl`) and additional text.

==== Gantry with continuous direction and traffic signs

image::ex-14.png[Gantry with continuous direction signs,width=600]

* VMS controller (`vms:VmsController`): 1×
